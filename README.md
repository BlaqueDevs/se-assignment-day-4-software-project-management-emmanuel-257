## 3. Compare and contrast Agile and Waterfall methodologies. What are the main advantages and disadvantages of each?
Agile;
advantages;
Faster response to changes.
Improved collaboration with stakeholders.
Early detection of defects.
disadvantages;
Requires active customer involvement.
Difficult to estimate time and costs accurately.
Waterfall;
advantage;
Clear structure and documentation.
Well-suited for projects with fixed requirements.
disadvantage;
Rigid and difficult to modify once started.
Late discovery of issues can lead to high rework costs.
Agile is iterative and incremental while waterfall is linear and sequential
Agile has frequent small releases while waterfall has one final release
## 4. In what types of projects might Agile be more beneficial than Waterfall, and vice versa? Can you provide examples of each?
Agile will be efficient in small software startups while waterfall will be efficient in large-scale enterprise systems.
